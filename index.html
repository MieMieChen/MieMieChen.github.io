About

I am an undergraduate researcher at the School of Computer Science, Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I study how long-horizon AI agents can be evaluated and improved when they operate in interactive environments or under deployment constraints.

I am currently a Research Assistant at UC San Diego's Q-Lab, working on reliable evaluation for coding agents that build interactive 3D worlds in SimWorld. I also conduct research at APEX Lab on deployment-aware reinforcement learning for low-bit language models.

Research interests

LLM AgentsAgent MemoryRecursive Self-ImprovementReinforcement LearningAgent EvaluationMultimodal RAGLow-bit Deployment

I am especially interested in agent memory and recursive self-improvement (RSI), and I am actively looking for research opportunities and collaborators in these directions.
